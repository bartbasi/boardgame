What changed (new facts, all in docs/api-research.md):

1. EBAY-27 (B14): the agreement's Restricted Activities forbid, without eBay's prior written consent, using eBay content "either alone or in combination with third-party information, to suggest or model prices for items listed on eBay Sites". Confirmed in the 2018 text; search results show it on the current page too. This was not in our research before.
2. EBAY-23: the full derived-statistics list is now confirmed (2018). Per-product asking-price statistics are not named in it.
3. EBAY-28: eBay content in a public display "must be visually isolated from third-party listings or other non-eBay information", and must be deleted once it is no longer public on eBay.
4. EBAY-29: we may let users see eBay content only under a binding user agreement that binds them to eBay's terms, names eBay a third-party beneficiary, and can be ended at any time.
5. EBAY-110 to EBAY-112 (B15): the data protection addendum (2018 text) requires a written security program, annual or per-release application security assessments for mobile apps that display eBay data, quarterly vulnerability scans, breach notice within 24 hours, destruction within 30 days once data is no longer needed, and "No eBay Data will be transmitted, stored or Processed in a non-production environment."
6. EBAY-113: a Q4 2025 clause on "Covered Persons and Transfers of Personal Data" exists (section 3.g.4 of the addendum); content not readable.
7. EBAY-114: no public statement about the eBay Developers Program without eBay's approval (2018 text).
8. EBAY-115: no use that may "undermine eBay's business interests", including services competitive to eBay.
9. EBAY-116: never collect or store eBay User IDs (2018 text). The PRD already keeps no seller identifiers in the listing cache.
10. EBAY-91: search results place the "withhold compensation for unapproved software" clause in the part on downloadable software applications. Mobile apps still need EPN approval (EBAY-90).
11. EBAY-90: EPN's mobile app form asks for test credentials for a user account. Our app has no accounts.
