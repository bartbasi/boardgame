## Proposed decisions

For the orchestrator to record in docs/decisions.md if accepted:

1. BGG request queue: one request every 5 seconds [ASSUMPTION], configurable; up to 20 ids per thing request; exponential backoff with jitter on 429, 500, 503, and 202. Reason: BGG publishes no limit, and its wiki says 5 seconds suffices (BGG-20, BGG-21). Replaces the brief's 2 seconds.
2. eBay listing cache on the server: at most 6 hours. The app hides cached listings older than 6 hours, including offline. Reason: EBAY-20.
3. Subscribe to eBay marketplace account deletion notifications with a backend endpoint instead of opting out. Reason: we store some eBay data (EBAY-10, EBAY-11).
4. Host on Ampere A1 (linux/arm64), within 2 OCPUs and 12 GB in total. Reason: OCI-01, OCI-02.
5. Integrity: verify App Attest or Play Integrity when a session starts and issue a short-lived signed server token for later requests, instead of checking every request. Reason: Play Integrity's 10,000 per day default quota (GPLAY-07, GPLAY-08). Pending U8.
6. Marketplace Insights stays off for v1, and v1 copy assumes asking prices only. Reason: EBAY-100.
7. The affiliate disclosure uses an EPN-approved phrase and sits next to the listings, visible without scrolling. Reason: EBAY-92, EBAY-93. Pending U5.

Places where the current PRD and backlog drafts conflict with the research. The T-001 author or the orchestrator should update them:

2. PRD section 6 rule 6 (stale marker at 48 hours) and section 8.2 (show cached data offline) conflict with eBay's content age limits: listings at most 6 hours old, other eBay content at most 24 hours old (EBAY-20).
